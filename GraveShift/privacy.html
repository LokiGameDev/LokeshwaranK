Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Grave Shift ("the Game").

Grave Shift is an independently developed multiplayer game.

Information We Collect

Grave Shift does not operate its own online database and does not intentionally collect or store personal information on our own servers.

The Game uses Unity Gaming Services for certain multiplayer and account-related features, including:

  • Unity Authentication
  • Unity Lobby
  • Unity Relay
  • Unity Friends

These services may process information necessary to provide their respective features.

The Game may also store certain game settings and gameplay-related information locally on your device.

Local Data

Some game data and settings may be stored locally on your device.

This may include things such as:

  • Game settings
  • Audio preferences
  • Graphics preferences
  • Gameplay/progression data
  • Other information required for the Game to function

This locally stored information is not stored by us on our own servers.

Multiplayer

Grave Shift uses Unity Gaming Services to provide multiplayer functionality.

Information required for multiplayer features may be processed by Unity and its services in accordance with their applicable policies and terms.

Game Updates

Grave Shift may use GitHub Releases to provide game updates.

When checking for or downloading an update, the Game may communicate with GitHub's services. GitHub may process information such as network or technical information according to its own privacy practices.

Advertising and Payments

Grave Shift currently:

  • Does not contain an advertising system.
  • Does not currently process in-game payments.
  • Does not currently require payment information from players.

The in-game shop is currently non-monetized and does not process real-world purchases.

Third-Party Services

Grave Shift uses third-party services including:

  • Unity Gaming Services
  • GitHub Releases

These services have their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

Children's Privacy

Grave Shift does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that the Game has collected personal information from a child, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Game and its features change.

Any updated version will include a revised "Last Updated" date.
